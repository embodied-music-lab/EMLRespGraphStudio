How these numbers are worked out

Time between packets is the gap between arrivals on this computer (performance.now). The strap-clock rate is samples divided by the strap's own timestamps; the computer-clock rate is the same samples divided by arrival times. Both skip the interval across a lost packet, a clock reset or a reconnect. A packet counts as lost when the strap timestamp jumps by more than 1.5 packet periods. Heart rate packets carry no strap timestamp, so only the computer-clock rate exists and losses cannot be counted. Heart rate notifications are always on while connected; the Heart rate box controls only whether this page records them.

Tap test (experimental): how large is the delay on this computer?

Turn the accelerometer on, press Start listening (the browser asks for the microphone), then tap the strap firmly 5 to 10 times next to the computer's microphone, about a second apart. The page finds each tap in the microphone and in the accelerometer and compares the two times. The result is the total offset of this computer's path: the Bluetooth link plus the audio input path. It cannot separate the two.
